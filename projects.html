From national environmental risk observatories to maternal health apps, we design, build, and support the digital platforms our clients rely on. Here is a look at some of the work we're proud of.

Environmental GIS Platform

GERO Ghana Environmental Risk Observatory

GERO is an open-access web platform that tracks environmental risks floods, droughts, soil erosion, land cover change, and urban heat across all 16 regions and 260+ districts in Ghana, using live satellite data.

  • Live Satellite Data: Combines multiple public data sources into one interactive map tool.
  • District Analytics: View localized environmental data down to regional and subdistrict levels.
  • Risk Tracking: Focused indicators for rainfall impact, drought indices, and soil loss.

Social Networking Platform

GFELTP Nexus

The GFELTP Nexus is a social networking and management platform developed for the Ghana Field Epidemiology and Laboratory Training Programme. It tracks program alumni, deploys current residents and graduates, shares job openings, and serves as a communication hub for public health professionals.

  • Alumni Tracking: Maintains a database of field epidemiology graduates.
  • Deployment & Jobs: Coordinates assignments and shares career opportunities.
  • Professional Networking: Connects public health workers to improve disease surveillance and response.

Health GIS & Mobile App

SafeMother+

SafeMother+ is a mobile tool built for community health workers, midwives, and clinical teams to monitor pregnant and lactating mothers, track risk levels, and send real-time alerts before complications turn into emergencies.

  • Quick Registration: Add new pregnant or lactating mothers in minutes.
  • Automatic Risk Scoring: Flag patients instantly under categories like Safe, Warning, or High Risk.
  • Real-Time Alerts: Notify care teams immediately when a patient's condition changes.
  • One-Tap Calling: Connect directly with the mother or her emergency contact.
  • Record Replacement: Replaces scattered paper files with a single digital system for remote and local clinics.
